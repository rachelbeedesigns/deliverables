TBT's Adult Ballet Nutcracker is free, and we'd love to see you there.

The studio holds 100 seats per night, shared among more than 40 dancers and their guests, so we can't promise everyone a seat. The form is a request, not a ticket. We'll email everyone by November 23 with confirmed tickets.

How it works

  1. By Nov 18Send one request per household. Tell us which dancer you are coming to see, which nights work, and how many seats you need.
  2. By Nov 23We email every requester with confirmed tickets, or let you know we couldn't find room.
  3. After thatReply to confirm. Seats that aren't confirmed go to the waitlist.

Plans changed?

After you submit, you get an email copy of your request with a link to edit it. If you lose it, send the form again and note the change in the last question.
